How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

Roof leaks, ponding water on flat roofs, damaged flashing or interior water stains all point to a problem. An inspection tells us whether a repair will resolve it or a full replacement is the better long-term answer.

Do you provide emergency roofing services in Brooklyn?

Yes. Goldenberg Roofing Brooklyn provides 24/7 emergency roofing for leaks, storm damage and unexpected roof failures. Call the office and we come out the same day.

Can I request a free estimate?

Yes. Send the form or call, and we inspect the property and give you an itemized free estimate with no obligation.

How quickly will I hear back?

We review every request within the hour and call you back to discuss the project and book a free inspection. If water is coming in right now, call (718) 360-5527 instead of waiting for a reply.

Which Brooklyn neighbourhoods do you cover?

All of Brooklyn, including Bushwick, Williamsburg, Greenpoint, Bedford-Stuyvesant, Park Slope, Crown Heights, Brooklyn Heights, Flatbush, Bay Ridge, Sunset Park and Prospect Heights, plus Ridgewood on the Queens border. Not sure we reach you? Call and ask.

Goldenberg Roofing Brooklyn is a licensed and insured roofing contractor for brownstones, row houses, apartment buildings and commercial properties across Brooklyn, with 35+ years of roofing experience in New York City. Free estimates and 24/7 emergency service.
