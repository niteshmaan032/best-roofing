Our process

How We Run Bronx Projects

Attached homes, apartment buildings, and tenants to plan around. The same four steps keep every Bronx job on track.

  1. Clear scope first

    We inspect the roof and lay out the scope and schedule for owners and managers.

  2. Access & scheduling

    We plan roof access, material hoisting, and work hours around tenants and neighbours.

  3. Trained crews

    Licensed, insured crews install every system and secure the roof at the end of each day.

  4. Final walkthrough

    We walk the finished roof with you or the super, check the drains, and clean up.
