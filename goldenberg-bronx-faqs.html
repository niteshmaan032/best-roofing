1. Roofing in the Bronx

Building types, roof systems, and the neighbourhoods we serve.

6 questionsContents

What roofing systems are common on Bronx buildings?

Flat (low-slope) roofs are the most common, because most Bronx row houses, apartment buildings, and commercial properties have flat roofs. We install and repair TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen (SBS) systems built for durability and good drainage, plus shingles where a building has a pitched roof.

Do you work on apartment buildings?

Yes. We work on apartment building and commercial roofs, including flat roofing projects. Each job is planned around the structure, roof access, and safety requirements of the building.

Do you work on row houses and attached homes?

Yes. Most row houses and attached homes have a flat roof behind a parapet, reached by a roof hatch or bulkhead. We repair and replace these roofs and the flashing at parapets, chimneys, skylights, and shared walls.

Do you work on older and prewar buildings?

Yes. We work on prewar apartment buildings and older homes as well as newer construction. Older roofs often hide several layers of past work, original parapets, and masonry that need care, so we tailor the approach to the building.

Do you work on commercial and mixed-use properties?

Yes. We roof commercial and mixed-use buildings, including storefronts with apartments above. Each project is planned around the building’s requirements and the businesses and tenants inside.

Which Bronx neighbourhoods do you serve?

We work across the Bronx, including Kingsbridge, Fordham, University Heights, Riverdale, Morris Heights, Bedford Park, Norwood, Mott Haven, Pelham Bay, Throgs Neck, Parkchester, and Morris Park. Our office is at 2430 Davidson Ave.

2. Apartment Buildings & Roof Access

Owners, managing agents, roof access, rooftop equipment, and bulkheads.

6 questionsContents

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. We lay out the scope, materials, schedule, and cost so owners and managing agents can review and approve the work, and we answer their questions before anything starts.

Do you coordinate with building management and the super?

Yes. We work with managing agents and supers on roof access, work hours, resident notices, and approvals, so the project runs smoothly for everyone in the building.

How do crews and materials get onto the roof?

We plan access with building management before work starts: how crews reach the roof (stairs, roof hatch, or bulkhead), how materials and debris move without damaging hallways, and which hours work is allowed. Every building is different, so access is part of the estimate.

Can you fix leaks around rooftop equipment and skylights?

Yes. Leaks often start where the roof meets HVAC curbs, ductwork, skylights, and drains. We find the source, then repair the flashing and membrane around it so the roof is watertight again.

Do you repair bulkheads and other rooftop structures?

Yes. Stair bulkheads, parapets, and equipment curbs are common leak points where walls meet the roof. We repair the flashing, coping, and membrane around them, and the masonry when the walls need it.

Will the roof work disrupt tenants?

We plan for minimal disruption to tenants and residents: agreed work hours, protected common areas, and a tidy roof at the end of each day. Noisy work such as a tear-off is scheduled with the owner or manager in advance.

3. Inspections & Repairs

When to inspect, what to watch for, and what to do about a leak.

6 questionsContents

How often should a Bronx roof be inspected?

At least once a year, plus after severe weather such as heavy storms, high winds, or a hard freeze. Regular inspections catch small problems early and keep the building safe and protected.

What are the signs a roof needs repair?

Leaks, water stains on top-floor ceilings, cracks or blisters in the membrane, water standing on a flat roof after rain, and damaged shingles or loose flashing are the common signs. An inspection shows whether a repair is enough.

What are the most common roofing problems in the Bronx?

Leaks, drainage problems, and membrane damage. Bronx roofs take heavy rain, snow, and summer heat, and foot traffic around rooftop equipment and freeze-thaw cycles add wear over time.

Do you inspect the roof before recommending repairs?

Yes. We inspect first so the roof repair fixes the actual cause of the problem, not just the symptom. You get clear options based on what we find.

Do you handle emergency roof repairs?

Yes. Call (718) 663-4786 about an active leak and we’ll talk through the emergency repair and the next step. Acting quickly limits damage to the rooms and apartments below. We’re open 24/7.

Do you offer roof maintenance?

Yes. Routine maintenance, such as clearing drains, resealing flashing, and fixing small wear, extends the life of flat roofs and shingle roofs and reduces long-term repair costs.

4. Gutters, Siding & Masonry

Keeping water off the walls and out of the building.

6 questionsContents

Do you install gutters and roof drainage?

Yes. We install and maintain gutters, leaders, and roof drainage for Bronx homes and buildings. Larger roofs collect more water, so their drainage has to be sized and routed for it.

Can poor drainage damage a building?

Yes. Water that pools on the roof or spills over the edge damages the walls, masonry, and structure over time. Clogged gutters and drains also back water up under the roof and cause leaks.

Do you provide masonry services?

Yes. We offer masonry repair, repointing, and restoration for brick and stone, including parapets and chimneys, to keep the structure sound and watertight.

What causes masonry damage?

Weather exposure, freeze-thaw cycles, and water getting into mortar joints all weaken masonry over time. Regular maintenance and timely repointing prevent deterioration.

Do you install and repair siding?

Yes. Along with roofing, we install and repair siding, gutters, and masonry, so the walls of your home or building stay dry from the roof down.

Can masonry problems affect safety?

Yes. Loose or cracked masonry on parapets and walls can put people below and the structure at risk, so timely repairs are essential.

5. Working With Us

Estimates, timelines, materials, and the standard we work to.

6 questionsContents

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Call (718) 663-4786 to set up an inspection and a free estimate for your home or building.

How long does a roofing project take?

It depends on the roof’s size and complexity and, for apartment buildings, on roof access and scheduling with management. You get a timeline with the estimate, and we work efficiently without cutting corners.

What roofing materials do you use?

Durable materials suited to city conditions: TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen (SBS) on flat roofs, and asphalt shingles on pitched roofs. The right choice depends on the building, how the roof is used, and the budget.

How do you ensure quality work?

Trained, licensed, and insured crews, proven installation methods, and close attention to the flashing, seams, and drains where most roofs fail.

Do you offer long-term solutions?

Yes. We focus on durability and performance, so the roof needs less upkeep and protects the building’s value and safety for years.
