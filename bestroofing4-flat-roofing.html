Repair Or Replace

Does your flat roof need a repair or a new roof?

Isolated damage can usually be repaired. When problems keep coming back, a new roof is often the better investment.

Usually a repair

The roof is sound overall

  • A leak in one area
  • Loose or worn flashing
  • A puncture or small membrane crack
  • A blocked drain or scupper

Time to replace

Repairs no longer hold

  • Leaks in different areas of the building
  • Membrane breaking down across the roof
  • Repair costs rising year after year
  • A roof near the end of its service life

Not sure which?

We inspect first and base the recommendation on the roof’s actual condition, never on sales pressure.

Common Problems

Where Manhattan flat roofs usually leak

Most leaks start where the roof meets something else. We find the source instead of patching the spot where water shows.

  • Parapet walls

    Where the roof meets masonry, aging flashing opens gaps.

  • Drains and scuppers

    Blocked or damaged drains leave water sitting on the roof.

  • Membrane seams

    Seams split as the roof expands and contracts each season.

  • Vents and HVAC

    Every pipe and equipment curb needs sound flashing.

  • Skylights

    The edge where a skylight meets the roof is a frequent entry point.

  • Ponding water

    Standing water weakens the membrane and speeds up wear.

Early warning signs

Damage is not always visible from the ground. On an inspection we look for:

  • Cracks and blistering
  • Punctures from debris or equipment
  • Pooling water after rain
  • Water stains on interior ceilings

All roofing FAQs

What materials are used for flat roofs in NYC?

Common flat roofing materials include EPDM rubber roofing, TPO membranes, and modified bitumen systems. Each offers different benefits depending on the building and its roofing needs.

What causes flat roof leaks?

Most leaks come from damaged membranes, poor drainage, standing water, or aging materials. Regular inspections catch these problems before they lead to major repairs.

How long does a flat roof last in New York City?

It depends on the material and how well the roof is maintained. Most flat roofing systems last between 20 and 30 years when properly installed and maintained.

How often should a flat roof be inspected?

At least once a year and after major storms. Regular inspections find damage early and extend the life of the roof.

Yes. Goldenberg Roofing NYC provides 24/7 emergency flat roof repair for leaks, storm damage, and unexpected roofing problems across New York City.
