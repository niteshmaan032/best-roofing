How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

Roof leaks, missing shingles, ponding water on flat roofs, damaged flashing or interior water stains all point to a problem. An inspection shows whether a repair will solve it or whether a full replacement is the better long-term answer.

What types of roofs are common on Brooklyn buildings?

Most Brooklyn brownstones, row houses, apartment buildings and commercial properties have flat roofing systems such as EPDM, TPO or modified bitumen. Detached homes often have asphalt shingle roofs. Brownstones and mixed-use buildings often need specialised details because of their parapets and drainage.

How often should a roof be inspected in Brooklyn?

At least once a year and after major storms. Regular inspections catch small issues like cracks, drainage problems or damaged materials before they become larger, more expensive repairs.

Do you provide emergency roofing services in Brooklyn?

Yes. Goldenberg Roofing Brooklyn is open 24/7 for leaks, storm damage and unexpected roof failures. A fast response protects the property from further structural damage and water getting inside.

Can I request a free estimate for roofing services?

Yes. We offer free estimates for roofing, flat roofing, siding, gutters and masonry. Call our Brooklyn team or send the form to schedule an inspection and receive a detailed estimate.
