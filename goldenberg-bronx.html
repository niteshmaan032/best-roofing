Flat vs. Shingle Roofing in the Bronx

Most Bronx row houses and apartment buildings have flat roofs. Pitched shingle roofs appear on detached homes, dormers, and smaller houses.

Low-slope · Most Bronx roofs

Flat Roofing

Waterproof membranes with tapered drainage and tight flashing at parapets, bulkheads, and rooftop equipment.

Typical buildings
Row houses, apartment buildings, and commercial properties
Materials
TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen (SBS) membranes
Watch for
Ponding water, membrane wear, and flashing leaks

Who is a reliable roofing contractor in the Bronx?

Goldenberg Roofing Bronx is a licensed and insured roofing contractor with more than 35 years of roofing experience in New York City, from flat roofs on row houses and apartment buildings to commercial properties.

What roofing services do you provide in the Bronx?

Roof repair, roof replacement, flat roofing, shingle roofing, and commercial roofing, plus siding, gutters, and masonry.

Do you specialize in flat roofing?

Yes. Flat roofing is our core service because most Bronx row houses and apartment buildings have flat roofs. We install and repair TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen systems.

How often should a roof be inspected in the Bronx?

At least once a year and after major storms. Regular inspections catch small issues like cracks, drainage problems, or damaged flashing before they become larger, more expensive repairs.

How do I know if my roof needs repair?

Leaks, water stains on top-floor ceilings, damaged shingles, and ponding water on flat roofs are common signs. We inspect the roof to find the cause before recommending a repair.
