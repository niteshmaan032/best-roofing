How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

Roof leaks, missing shingles, ponding water on flat roofs, damaged flashing or interior water stains all point to a problem. A professional inspection determines whether a repair will resolve it or whether a full replacement is the better long-term solution.

What types of roofs are common in NYC buildings?

Many residential and commercial buildings in NYC have flat roofing systems such as EPDM, TPO or modified bitumen. Residential homes may have asphalt shingle roofs. Brownstones and mixed-use buildings often need specialized solutions because of their structural design.

How often should a roof be inspected in New York City?

At least once a year and after major storms. Regular inspections catch small issues like cracks, drainage problems or damaged materials before they become larger, more expensive repairs.

Do you provide emergency roofing services in NYC?

Yes. Goldenberg Roofing NYC provides 24/7 emergency roofing for leaks, storm damage and unexpected roof failures. Fast response protects the property from further structural damage and interior water intrusion.

Can I request a free estimate for roofing services?

Yes. We offer free estimates for roofing, flat roofing, siding, gutters and masonry. Call our team directly or submit a request online to schedule an inspection and receive a detailed estimate.
