How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

Roof leaks, ponding water on flat roofs, damaged flashing or interior water stains all point to a problem. An inspection tells us whether a repair will resolve it or a full replacement is the better long-term answer.

Do you provide emergency roofing services in NYC?

Yes. Goldenberg Roofing NYC provides 24/7 emergency roofing for leaks, storm damage and unexpected roof failures. Call the office and we come out the same day.

Can I request a free estimate?

Yes. Send the form or call, and we inspect the property and put a written, itemized estimate in front of you at no charge and with no obligation.

How quickly will I hear back?

We review every request the same day and call you back to discuss the property and book an inspection. If water is coming in right now, call instead of waiting for the form.
