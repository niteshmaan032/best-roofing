Common problems

Where Bronx Flat Roofs Usually Leak

Most leaks start where the roof meets something else. We find the source instead of patching the spot where water shows.

Damage is not always visible from the street. On an inspection we look for these early warning signs:

  • Cracks and blistering
  • Punctures from debris
  • Pooling water after rain
  • Stains on top-floor ceilings

Repair or replace

Does Your Flat Roof Need a Repair or a New Roof?

Isolated damage can usually be repaired. When problems keep coming back, a new roof is often the better investment. Watch for these signs.

  • Leaks after stormsCeiling stains or damp spots on the top floor
  • Cracks and blisteringBubbles and splits in the membrane surface
  • PuncturesHoles from debris, tools, or rooftop equipment
  • Pooling waterWater still sitting on the roof after rain
  • Loose flashingGaps where the roof meets parapets and walls
  • Split seamsSeams opening as the roof moves with the seasons

Repair or Replace?

Usually a repair

  • The roof is sound overall
  • A leak in one area
  • Loose or worn flashing

Time to replace

  • Repairs no longer hold
  • Leaks in different areas
  • Membrane worn across the roof

Flat Roofing FAQs

What materials are used for flat roofs in the Bronx?

Common flat roofing materials include EPDM rubber roofing, TPO membranes, and modified bitumen (SBS) systems. Each offers different benefits depending on the building and its roofing needs.

What causes flat roof leaks?

Most leaks come from damaged membranes, poor drainage, standing water, or aging materials. Regular inspections catch these problems before they lead to major repairs.

How long does a flat roof last in New York City?

It depends on the material and how well the roof is maintained. Most flat roofing systems last between 20 and 30 years when properly installed and maintained.

How often should a flat roof be inspected?

At least once a year and after major storms. Regular inspections find damage early and extend the life of the roof.
