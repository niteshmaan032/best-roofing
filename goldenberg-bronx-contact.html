Common Questions About Getting Started

Can I request a free estimate?

Yes. Send the form or call, and we inspect the property and give you an estimate at no charge and with no obligation.

How quickly will I hear back?

We review every request and call you back within the hour to discuss the project and book a free inspection. If water is coming in right now, call (718) 663-4786 instead of waiting for a reply.

Do you provide emergency roofing in the Bronx?

Yes. Goldenberg Roofing Bronx is open 24/7 for leaks, storm damage, and unexpected roof failures. Call the office any time, day or night.

How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

Roof leaks, ponding water on flat roofs, damaged flashing, or interior water stains all point to a problem. An inspection tells us whether a repair will resolve it or a full replacement is the better long-term answer.

Which Bronx neighbourhoods do you cover?

All of the Bronx, including Riverdale, Kingsbridge, Fordham, University Heights, Morris Heights, Bedford Park, Norwood, Mott Haven, Parkchester, Morris Park, Pelham Bay, and Throgs Neck. Not sure we reach you? Call (718) 663-4786 and ask.
